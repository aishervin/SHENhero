Your coding workspace, running locally on Android. Keep the familiar coding UI and workflows, with the service and proxy bound to your own device.

Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aishervin/SHENhero/main/termux-skin/install.sh | bash

The installer prepares the local launcher and prebuilt UI, starts the local coding service, and opens the workspace in your browser. The local UI/API does not require a SHΞN ʜᴇʀᴏ™ hosted backend.

Connect an Android app

When the local server is running, compatible apps can connect to the configured loopback endpoint:

http://127.0.0.1:4096
